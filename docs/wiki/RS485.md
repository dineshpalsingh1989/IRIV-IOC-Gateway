# RS485 (Modbus RTU)

The controller polls Modbus RTU devices (meters, drives, sensors) on its RS485 port and republishes each value on Modbus TCP and MQTT. Up to 64 values.

## Wiring

A, B and G on terminal 1. Turn on the 120 Ω termination switch only on the two devices at the ends of the bus. Max 500 kbps.

## 1. Set the bus

Under **Connections → RS485**: baud rate, parity, stop bits, poll interval, timeout and the device word order for 32-bit values (ABCD, CDAB, BADC or DCBA). Turn **RS485 polling** on when you are ready.

## 2. Test the sensor

In **RS485 points**, enter the sensor's **slave ID** (1–247), **function** (FC03 holding, FC04 input, FC01 coil, FC02 discrete input), **register** (0-based: 40001 is 0), **data type** and **scale**, then click **Test read**.

The controller reads that register once with the bus settings on the page, even before you save them and while polling is off:

- **It answers:** "Slave 1 answered: 23.4 m³/h (raw 0x41BB 0x3333, float32)". Scale and word order are applied exactly as the firmware will.
- **It does not:** the reason (no reply, CRC error, Modbus exception) and what to check: slave ID, wiring, baud rate and parity.

Test read works on the controller's own page over the LAN, or on the website after **Connect over USB** on the Install page.

## 3. Add it

Click **Add point**. It gets automatically:

- a **Modbus TCP address** in the RS485 block: `0x1000`, `0x1002`, … (float32, two registers each). Existing addresses never move when you delete a point.
- an **MQTT topic**: `…/rs485/p1/state`, or one you choose (the form suggests free topics and refuses ones already in use).

Then save and send the settings to the controller.

## Values

- Each point is read once per poll interval.
- A value stays through two failed reads; after the third it becomes `NaN` on Modbus TCP and MQTT, so SCADA sees the loss.
- On the controller's own page the points table shows live values or **no reply**.
